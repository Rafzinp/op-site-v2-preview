Buying guide

Choosing eco bagasse containers

Bagasse is a sugarcane fibre that is biodegradable and compostable, a practical swap for plastic and foam take-away containers. Choose compartment trays for combo meals, deep bowls for soups and salads, and burger boxes for hot sandwiches. Every container pairs with a PET, PP or bagasse lid.

01Pick the format

Rectangle, square, bowl or burger box.

02Choose the size

From 280 ml to 1000 ml and 32 oz.

03Match the lid

PET, PP or bagasse lids.

Eco bagasse FAQs

Is bagasse packaging compostable?

Our eco bagasse range is biodegradable and compostable. Ask our team about the right disposal route for your kitchen.
