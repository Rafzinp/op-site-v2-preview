How to choose the right paper cup size for your café

19 Nov 2026·6 min read·By the oslopack team

Choosing a cup size is less about volume and more about what your customers order, how fast the queue moves and how much you want to spend per serve.

Start with your menu

List your top five drinks and the volume of each. Espresso-based drinks usually sit at 4 to 8 oz, flat whites and lattes at 8 to 12 oz, and iced drinks at 12 to 20 oz.

Standardising on three sizes keeps stock simple and lets you buy lids and sleeves in bigger cartons at better prices.

Pick the wall type

Single-wall cups are the lowest cost. Double-wall and ripple cups keep hands cool without a sleeve, which saves money on sleeves and speeds up service.

Quick rule

Order hot cup lids with your cups: every cup size has a matching lid.

Match the lid

Always order lids with the cups. Our product pages list the matching lid for every size, and our team can confirm fit before you buy.
