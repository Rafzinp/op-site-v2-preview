Our story

The complete solutions for food packaging

Founded in 2011 by industry experts, Oslopack is a key source for brands and outlets that want to leave a positive, lasting impression on their customers. We set out to build a strong product distributorship in Doha, and through strategic partnerships with international suppliers across the GCC we now offer one of the largest collections of packaging products.

In 2017 we introduced our own brand, Oslopack, alongside the international brands we distribute.
