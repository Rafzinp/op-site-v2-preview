What types of packaging do you offer?−

Eco bagasse, kraft, microwavable and aluminium containers, paper cups, plastic and clear cups, wraps, paper bags, tissues, gloves and cleaning materials.

How do I place an order?−

Add products to your request list and send it. We reply within one working day with firm prices and a delivery date. There is no payment at this stage.

Is there a minimum order?−

Standard products are sold by the carton. Custom-printed bags and cups start from 500 pieces per design.

Can I mix products in one request?−

Yes. Mix any ranges in a single request. Free delivery applies on orders over 50 cartons.

Can I request samples before ordering?−

Yes. Ask our team for samples of any standard product, and for a printed sample before a custom run goes into production.

How do I reorder?−

Open My requests, choose a past order and select Reorder. Quantities and options are copied into a new request.
