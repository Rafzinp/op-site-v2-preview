Frequently asked questions

View all →
Which categories do you supply to hotels?−

Aluminium containers and foil, napkins and tissues, gloves, hygiene products, cleaning materials, dispensers, plus kraft, bagasse and microwavable containers.

Can you deliver to several venues?−

Yes. Set up multiple delivery addresses on one account and schedule monthly drops per venue, with a single invoice if you prefer.

What payment terms do you offer?−

Approved accounts can pay by bank transfer or LPO on net terms. Apply through the B2B portal and our team confirms the terms within two working days.

Can you print our hotel branding?−

Yes. Add your hotel or catering brand to bags, cups and boxes from 500 pieces, with a proof and a sample for approval before production.

Is there a minimum order for contract pricing?−

There is no fixed minimum, but contract pricing works best from around 50 cartons a month. Our account team sizes a plan around your volume.
