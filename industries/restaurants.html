Frequently asked questions

View all →
Are your containers microwave safe?−

Our kraft and bagasse containers are suitable for reheating. Each product page lists its temperature limits, so you can check before you order.

Do you stock tamper-evident options?−

Yes. Selected boxes and containers come with tamper-evident seals or stickers for delivery orders. Ask our team to include them in your quote.

Can I mix sizes in one order?−

Yes. You can mix sizes and products in a single quote. Pricing is per carton, so combine cartons across ranges to reach free delivery on orders over 50 cartons.

Do you deliver outside Doha?−

We deliver across Doha within 48 hours. For other cities in Qatar, our team confirms the delivery time in your quote.

Are the boxes and containers grease-proof?−

Our kraft boxes and bagasse containers are grease-resistant, so oily and saucy food stays contained. Window boxes use a food-safe film.
